OPEN-RECORD

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE exceptional original logo symbol for POLITIC PUBLIC, a local-government intelligence brand with the promise 'Come prepared' and the mission to make the public record useful to everyone. This asset is a concept mark only: NO text, NO letters, NO mockup, NO labels. Concept name: THE OPEN RECORD. Design an unforgettable compact sigil in which a pair of open document leaves form a sheltering open doorway in the negative space. Record and access become one shape. An intellectually satisfying double reading: spread pages viewed from above; open threshold viewed from front. Simple black silhouette with two or three intentional cutouts. Human, hand-cut feeling, slightly unequal lobes, subtly bowed edges, a tilted blunt terminal. Personality in the actual outline, never artificial distress or grunge. Quietly confident, warm, civic, contemporary independent design studio quality. Emblem-like but not a government seal, religious sign, or occult symbol. Do not use a generic open-book icon, bulb, eye, starburst, checkmark, or letter P. Do not emulate any existing brand mark. Dense, strong, carefully balanced silhouette with surprising asymmetric shape, readable at 24px and stampable in one ink. Flat ink navy #142b36 shape centered on solid warm paper #f4f1e8 background. Square canvas, symbol roughly 50 percent of image width and height, generous clear space. No texture, gradients, shadows, borders, or ornament. Each contour must feel considered and distinctive.
