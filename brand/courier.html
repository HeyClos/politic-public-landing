Ink color

The original Courier’s contours are preserved in a single-color vector. The wordmark is outlined, so every signature is independent of fonts.

Information, in public hands.

The document makes the bird’s purpose specific. It carries useful information from a public meeting into someone’s working day. That connects the professional product to the wider public mission.

Attentive. Capable. Approachable.

The upright tail and forward stance give it alertness. Its small, local character adds warmth to the calm authority of the type. It belongs comfortably beside a serious legislative brief.

Drawn with intention.

Three unequal tail feathers, three curved wing cuts, and wiry, staggered feet preserve the drawing’s character. Flat vector contours replace the raster grain; the slight asymmetries stay.

Use the full Courier at 48 px and above. Below 48 px, use the optical mark: wider tail gaps, a larger eye, one wing cut, stronger paper, and simpler feet. Favicons use a gold field for light and dark browser tabs.

The finished asset system

Ready for the public record.

Use the signature for first encounters and the standalone bird once the name is established. Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the bird’s height. Scale proportionally; preserve the orientation and open counters.

Ink on paper is primary. Use paper or ochre on ink for reversed applications. The full Courier and the optical mark serve different sizes; keep the full drawing for larger uses.
