POLITIC PUBLIC — THE COURIER
Final vector identity | 19 October 2026

THE IDEA
An attentive local courier carrying the public record into reach. The uneven
silhouette adds a human character to the product's promise of preparedness.

THE APPROVED DRAWING
The full Courier follows the original selected sketch: three unequal upright
tail feathers, three curved wing cuts, a round eye, wiry staggered feet, and an
outlined tilted document. The paper texture has been removed. The character
of the contour has been retained. The wordmark is Barlow Condensed 700,
converted to outlines in all final signatures.

COLOR
Ink #142b36 | Paper #f4f1e8 | Ochre #e4b95b | Green #47675d
Use ink on paper, or paper/ochre on ink. Preserve the open counters.

USAGE
Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the visible bird's height.
Scale proportionally. Keep the right-facing orientation. Do not redraw the
feathers, replace the paper with a solid bag, add facial expressions, or
distress the vector to imitate raster texture. Use the optical version when
the small spaces stop reading clearly.

SOURCE AND REBUILD
Editable SVG masters: brand/source/courier-master.svg and
brand/source/courier-small-master.svg. The full source was traced to cubic
vector contours from the selected ImageGen sketch with VTracer; the optical
source was drawn separately for small-size clarity. Both masters are paths.

Build derived SVGs with scripts/build-brand.py (fonttools + brotli).
Export PNG and ICO with scripts/render-brand.cjs (sharp).
The outlined wordmark retains the licensed typeface's shapes. See the
included Barlow Condensed license. The website's body font is Public Sans.

STATUS
Applied to the local landing-page package. No production deployment.
